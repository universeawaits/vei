The Workshops

Class Topics

Two teacher couples, two distinct programs. Here are the class topics and the time of each one — every class is at Dom omladine.

Vlasta Veselko & Relja Dereta

  1. 01

    Vals

    Int
    Sat 31 Oct · 13:30–14:50

    Technique and sequences to make your dance flow.

  2. 02

    Creativity in small spaces

    Int / Adv
    Sun 1 Nov · 12:00–13:20

    Sequences and embellishments for social dance.

Noelia Hurtado & Sebastian Fernandez

  1. 01

    Individual technique for men and women

    All levels
    Fri 30 Oct · 17:00–18:20

    Leg strength, connection to the floor, alignment, balance, different movement qualities, and linear movements.

  2. 02

    Different types of turns

    Int / Adv
    Fri 30 Oct · 18:30–19:50

    Synchronization and possibilities.

  3. 03

    Rhythm

    Int / Adv
    Sat 31 Oct · 12:00–13:20

    Interpretation, technique, structure, rhythmic variations.

  4. 04

    Melody

    Int / Adv
    Sat 31 Oct · 15:00–16:20

    Interpretation, different movement qualities, and tools for expression.

  5. 05

    Milonga

    Int
    Sun 1 Nov · 13:30–14:50

    Technique, milonga lisa and traspié. Structures and rhythmical variation for the dance floor.

  6. 06

    Circularity within the couple

    Int
    Sun 1 Nov · 15:00–16:20

    Pivots, ochos, voleos, and different movement qualities.
