Two communities, one embrace

It takes two to tango!

These two truly move as one – and isn't that exactly what we strive for in tango? Each stands firmly on their own feet, yet they come together in an embrace, united by a shared mission and vision for the development of the tango community across the region and beyond.

Tango Natural Belgrade may appear to be the older sibling, but the truth is that everything the Banjaluka team has built over the past 11 years is equally worthy of respect – and of your visit.

Discover the Banjaluka tango community and fall in love with its cozy dancing experience, charming city, and the wonderful people who will welcome you.

The Venue

The Stone House

Experience Day and Gran milongas in the cozy venue of the Stone House, nestled within the historic walls of Kastel Fortress, the oldest landmark of Banjaluka. Surrounded by ancient stone and the gentle flow of the Vrbas River, this unique venue carries the spirit of centuries past – from Roman settlements to medieval fortifications and Ottoman heritage.

As autumn colours settle over the fortress and the riverbank, the Stone House reveals its special charm. Golden leaves, timeless architecture and the quiet presence of history create an atmosphere both intimate and inspiring – a rare opportunity to gather, celebrate and create new memories in a setting shaped by centuries.

The Workshops

Workshops at Dom omladine

Discover workshops at Dom omladine, a welcoming venue in the heart of Banjaluka. Just a short five-minute walk from Kastel Fortress, where all daytime and evening milongas take place, it creates a natural connection between learning and dancing throughout the festival.

Walk through the lively city centre, enjoy the atmosphere of Banjaluka and return inspired to the dance floor. With everything within easy reach, the festival invites you to experience not only great tango, but also the charm, warmth and relaxed spirit of the city itself.

The Rhythm

Marathon spirit, festival soul.

THUPre-Party
FRIEvening Milonga
SATDay Milonga
SATEvening Milonga
SUNDay Milonga
SUNAfter-Party

From Friday evening until Sunday afternoon, the festival flows through four milongas that blend into one unforgettable tango journey. Two evening milongas and two daytime gatherings create that special marathon feeling we all love – the joy of dancing, reconnecting with friends, and losing track of time in the embrace of great music and warm embraces.

And for those who wish to immerse themselves a little deeper, the experience begins with a welcoming Pre-Party on Thursday and gently winds down with an After-Party on Sunday evening. A chance to meet the local community, enjoy the relaxed rhythm of Banjaluka, and savour every moment before returning home.

The Meeting Point

Experience the spirit of a region through tango.

BelgradeZagrebLjubljanaSarajevoNovi SadZenica···BANJALUKA

With Belgrade, Zagreb, Ljubljana, Sarajevo, Novi Sad and many other cities just a few hours away, Banjaluka stands at the crossroads of a region whose people remain deeply connected through culture, mentality and a shared love of gathering together.

The festival offers a rare opportunity to experience the tango communities of this part of Europe in one place – their diversity, their warmth and the unique social energy that emerges when they come together.

If you want to understand what makes tango in this region so special, this is the place to experience it.
