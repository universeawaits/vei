General Info

A festival built around the dance,
not the stage.

Every spring, dancers from across Europe and beyond gather in Lyon for four days of workshops, milongas, and live orchestras. The format stays close to the tradition it celebrates: small classes, a proper wooden floor, and evenings that run long.

Days are split between technique-focused workshops with visiting couples and open social dancing after dark — no performances-only lineup, no rushed rotations. The festival is organised with TNT Lyon Tango, the local school that keeps the city's tango scene running year-round.
